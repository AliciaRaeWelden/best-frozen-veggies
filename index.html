Skillet corn with paprika & vinegar

Sweet, savory, a little tangy. The first batch tasted good. The oil measurement was less successful.

What I learned: Cook off the water first; measure the oil next time.

Read the recipe + notes

Ingredients

  • About 12 oz frozen corn
  • A little cooking oil (quantity not yet standardized)
  • Paprika, garlic powder, and salt, to taste
  • A splash of vinegar

Method

  1. Place frozen corn in a wide, dry skillet over medium to medium-high heat. Cook uncovered until most water evaporates.
  2. Add a little oil; cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through and lightly golden in places.
  3. Season with paprika, garlic powder, and salt.
  4. Finish with vinegar; taste and adjust.

First-trial note: The corn was good but a bit oily. The oil quantity wasn't measured carefully. Timing and amounts remain provisional.

Skillet study · Tested once

Pan-browned mixed vegetables

A better texture for the everyday freezer mix. The trick is getting rid of the water before asking the vegetables to brown.

What I learned: Moisture matters. Seasoning still needs a little work.

Read the recipe + notes

Ingredients

  • About 12 oz frozen mixed vegetables
  • A little cooking oil (quantity not yet standardized)
  • Garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper
  • Optional: lemon juice or vinegar to finish

Method

  1. Cook frozen vegetables uncovered in a wide, dry skillet over medium to medium-high heat until most moisture evaporates.
  2. Add a little oil and continue cooking until the vegetables develop some color.
  3. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper to taste.
  4. Optionally finish with lemon juice or vinegar.

First-trial note: Texture worked well; flavor could be bolder. Exact amounts and timing still need testing.

♡ Ingredients

  • 1 fennel bulb, sliced thin
  • Bok choy, chopped; stems and leaves separated
  • Cubed tofu
  • ¼ cup cashews
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Chili flakes

✿ Upgrade

  • Edamame
  • Bell pepper

♡ Instructions

  1. Crisp tofu in a pan, then remove.
  2. Toast cashews, then remove.
  3. Cook fennel for 5–7 minutes, until golden.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, and bok choy stems; cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Add bok choy leaves and cook until wilted.
  6. Add soy sauce, vinegar or lime juice, maple syrup, and chili flakes. Return tofu and cashews to the pan and mix everything together.
  7. Drizzle with sesame oil.

A note from the kitchen

Why frozen veggies?

A very small obsession
with a very ordinary food.

I think eating more vegetables should be easy, affordable, and genuinely enjoyable.

Frozen vegetables last a long time and require almost no planning. But there's a difference between vegetables that are merely convenient and vegetables you're excited to eat.

I'm testing small changes and sharing what works, what doesn't, and what I'm still figuring out. Think of this as a public kitchen notebook. You're welcome to cook along.

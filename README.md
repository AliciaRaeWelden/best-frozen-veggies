## An open recipe notebook

**These are version 0.1 recipes, each tested successfully once.** They're shared early on purpose, rather than waiting for a polished collection. Measurements and timing may change with further testing. Please use your judgment, taste as you go, and adjust to your ingredients and stove.

Each recipe includes what worked, what still needs improvement, and what to try next. Future versions will document refinements, additional recipes, and the cooking science behind them.

The goal is to help more people make tasty everyday vegetables without expensive ingredients, specialized equipment, or a lot of extra work.

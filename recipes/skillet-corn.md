# Skillet Corn with Paprika and Vinegar

**Version 0.1 — tested once.** This is an early, successful trial, not a fully standardized recipe.

## Ingredients
- Frozen corn (about 12 oz)
- A little cooking oil (amount not precisely measured in the first trial)
- Paprika
- Garlic powder
- Salt
- A splash of vinegar

## Method
1. Add frozen corn to a wide, dry skillet over medium to medium-high heat. Cook uncovered until the released water mostly evaporates.
2. Add a little oil and continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until heated through and lightly browned in spots.
3. Add paprika, garlic powder, and salt to taste.
4. Finish with a splash of vinegar, taste, and adjust.

## Notes from the first trial
The result was tasty and mostly yellow, rather than deeply browned. It came out somewhat oily because the oil wasn't measured accurately.

**Next experiment:** Measure the oil carefully, starting with a smaller amount, and see whether the corn stays flavorful without feeling oily. Exact quantities and timing will be refined after additional trials.

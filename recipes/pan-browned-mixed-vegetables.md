# Pan-Browned Mixed Vegetables

**Version 0.1 — tested once.** This is an early successful trial, not a fully standardized recipe.

## Ingredients
- Frozen mixed vegetables (about 12 oz)
- A little cooking oil (not precisely measured in the first trial)
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Salt and pepper
- Optional squeeze of lemon or splash of vinegar to finish (not yet standardized)

## Method
1. Add the frozen vegetables to a wide, dry skillet over medium to medium-high heat. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until most of the released moisture evaporates.
2. Add a little oil and continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables develop a bit of color.
3. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper to taste.
4. Optionally finish with a little lemon juice or vinegar. Taste and adjust.

## Notes from the first trial
The mixed-vegetable skillet came out well. Cooking off the moisture first was useful for the texture, though the flavor could be more prominent.

**Next experiment:** Measure the oil and seasonings and compare different finishing acids. Quantities and timing are deliberately provisional until repeated testing.
